*Password* b0.85 has no display of the six main characters' total affection and no individual point-gain prompts. Dialogue, interactions, and the menus that appear provide observable signs of the relationship.

This page lists choices by date and provides routes for romance, friendship, and replaying story variations. Compare outcomes from earlier saves; later intimacy usually cannot restore an earlier missed scene.

::: {.callout-important}
## What affection controls—and what it does not

Character relationships affect internal narration, short dialogues, hugs and kisses, some CGs, and whether romance begins on D19.

These relationship choices do not directly decide lettered Paths, Vault password results, character survival, medals, or Path P. Follow the corresponding walkthroughs for those requirements.
:::

## Core behavior

::: {.pw-rule-groups}

**Saves and replaying variations**

- Normal story saves retain the relationship progress at that date. Continue the choices from that date after loading an earlier save.
- Keep saves at D3, before the D5 movie, D6 character event, D7 family topic, D8 library, and D19 relationship conversation.

**Menus and prerequisites**

- The D4 maze partner selects the character route; later character-specific menus require the relevant route.
- Choose one D5 movie and one family topic after D7 `Stay.`. Some topics also affect interactions with other characters.
- These plans assume D1 cup name `Chase` and no D3 `THE LOVERS`, except where explicitly stated. Additional relationship Easter eggs or different cross-character topics can change whether a relationship menu appears.
- The D6 practice kiss and Tyson rescue require a successful Vault check that day; the D15–D16 plans apply to Path A/B. Certain D18 variations also require the D17 Vault check to succeed. See the password and Path guides for those choices.

:::

## Hoss CG planning {#hoss-threshold-planning}

The two kiss CGs appear separately on D8 and D19. This plan needs neither a D1 relationship cup name nor D3 `THE LOVERS`.

### D8 hidden-library kiss CG

1. D2 `Pilates?`; message `Hoss` on D3; choose Hoss as the D4 maze partner, then `Stay.`.
2. D5 `You can pick.`; first performance response **Oh! Thank god you're here!**, second response `I... Who {i}are{/i} you?` (*are* is italicized in the game); dinner `Hoss`, movie `Action`.
3. D6 workout `Hoss.`, then `Guys like me?` → `Neither.`.
4. D7 `Good!`, then `Stay.` → `Hoss.`.
5. Enter the D8 hidden library on the Hoss Route to see the formal kiss and CG, then choose `Hold his hand`.

Alternatively, change the first D5 performance response to `Am I in danger?` and the second to `Oh no! Not Slimes!`, keeping the other choices. These are two successive menus; the answers within each are mutually exclusive.

::: {.pw-key-rule}

Save before the D8 library. If no formal kiss occurs there, Hoss can still kiss Dave on D9 night, but that will not unlock the missed D8 CG in that run.

:::

### D19 Hoss relationship CG

Continue the plan with D9 companion `Hoss.`, character-event response `Answer.`, and night response `...hope you sleep well.`. D19 offers `Try dating.` / `Stay friends.`.

**`Try dating.`** begins the relationship and displays the D19 Hoss relationship CG. `Stay friends.` retains friendship. No additional relationship Easter egg or automatic relationship outcome is required.

## Character choice guide {#complete-point-list}

Follow each character's plan first. The tables add alternative choices, dates, and prerequisites. Menu text remains in English; choices within one menu are mutually exclusive. Automatic story events need no extra action.

In the Use column, **Pick** means an interactive choice, **Auto** an automatic story event, **Egg** an optional Easter egg, and **Back** a CG checkpoint that may require an earlier save.

Use an earlier save on the relevant character route and follow the plans above to replay Sal's intimacy and D19 outcomes. See the [lettered Path guide](../guide/path-system.md) for Path requirements.

### The D16 `Remain still.` interaction {#d16-remain-still.-awards-no-points}

When D16 on the Sal Route offers `Remain still.` / `Hug.`, the game displays no numerical point-gain prompt.

- `Remain still.`: wait for Sal to approach and hug Dave, then lift him, with playful dialogue.
- `Hug.`: Dave initiates; Sal hugs back and pats his head, emphasizing mutual understanding and comfort.

::: {.callout-warning}
## Do not use this D16 menu to repair a relationship plan

`Remain still.` does not advance the relationship further. Arrange the D19 outcome around D9; within the plans above, either D16 option can be used to replay the interaction.
:::
